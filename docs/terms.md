"Bot"). The Bot is operated privately (the "Operator") and is not a

## 1. What the Bot does

The Bot is installed in a single, explicitly configured Discord server (the
"Server"). Within that Server, and only there, it sets the **server
nickname** of every member it is permitted to manage to a single shared
name (by default, "We"), and restores that nickname whenever it changes.

The Bot does not read or send messages, does not assign or remove roles,
and does not moderate. It changes server nicknames only. Members whom
Discord does not allow the Bot to manage — the Server owner and members
whose highest role is at or above the Bot's — are left untouched.

## 2. Acceptance

in which the Bot operates, you acknowledge that your server nickname in
that Server will be managed by the Bot as described above.

If you do not want your nickname managed, you may ask a Server
administrator to place your role above the Bot's role or to remove the
Bot, or you may leave the Server. Your Discord account-wide name is never
affected.
